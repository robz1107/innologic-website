Questions we often hear

Do you have a point of sale for restaurants and cafés?

Yes. The restaurant POS has a table floor plan, orders with notes, a kitchen screen and station tickets, split and merged bills, several payment methods, shifts and a cash drawer. Each dish has a recipe, so ingredients leave stock when you sell. Accounting, purchasing and tax are in the same system.

Can guests order from their phone at the table?

Yes. Every table has a QR code. Guests scan it, choose from the menu and send the order, and your staff accept it before it goes to the kitchen or bar. No app or login is needed. On your restaurant Wi-Fi it works on your own network. For guests on mobile data, the system needs an internet address (HTTPS), which we help you set up.

Does it connect to GoFood, GrabFood and ShopeeFood?

Not automatically yet. The cashier types the order from the platform's tablet. The system then books the sale, works out the commission, records what the platform owes you, and matches each payout to its orders. Automatic import is not available today.

Which printers and devices does the restaurant POS work with?

It runs in a browser on a PC, laptop or tablet. Kitchen, bar and receipt tickets can print on 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printers over your network (for example Epson TM-T82 or TM-T88, Xprinter, Sunmi), on a USB printer shared from the server PC, or through the normal browser print dialog. We test your printers during setup, and the receipt printer can open the cash drawer.

Can it add service charge and VAT to the bill?

Yes. You can set service charge, packing or delivery fees by type of bill, and the VAT percentage (11% by default). Paid bills keep the percentages they were paid with. Please confirm the right rates with your tax advisor.

Do we need cloud hosting or constant internet?

No. Innologic runs on a computer or small server in your office, and your staff use it in their browser over your network. Secure remote access can be arranged if you need it.

Can it handle several branches or companies?

Yes. Branches and departments can be tracked separately, and each company keeps its own books, with billing between companies and group statements.

Can we bring our existing products and customers?

Yes. We help import products, customers, vendors and opening balances so you do not start from zero.

Is our data safe?

Users only see what their role allows, changes are recorded in an activity log, and two-factor login is available. We set up regular backups and recommend a copy on a second drive or in cloud storage.

Is the tax output ready to file?

It records and exports the data you need for VAT, PPh and Coretax e-Faktur. Please confirm rates and filing details with your tax advisor.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your business and how much setup and training you need. Send us a message for a clear quote.

What the retail POS covers

Everything the counter needs, with the back office behind it.

A fast counter

Tap or scan products, keep several orders open at once, and change the quantity, price or discount in a tap. Items with a warranty take a serial number.

  • Barcode scanning and product search
  • Several open orders per cashier
  • Serial numbers and warranty lookup
  • Sessions with opening cash, cash in and out and a closing count

Payment, promotions and points

Split a sale over cash, card, QRIS and transfer. The best valid promotion is applied by itself, and a member can pay part of the sale with points.

  • Several payment methods on one sale
  • Promotions by day, hour, payment method or product group
  • Loyalty points used as a payment
  • Refunds approved with a supervisor PIN

Receipts and printers

Choose what your receipt says and see it before you save. Print on a 58 or 80 mm thermal printer over the network or by USB, and let the cash drawer open by itself.

  • Shop name, address, greeting and footer lines
  • Network, USB or browser printing
  • The cash drawer opens with the receipt
  • Every reprint is recorded

Loyalty members

Customers join at the counter or by scanning a QR code. You set the points, the tiers, the birthday bonus and how long a membership lasts.

  • Join with a QR code at the counter
  • Tiers such as Silver and Gold with bigger rewards
  • Birthday bonus points
  • Reports on points earned, used and expired

Promotions

A percentage off the whole sale or only some products, with dates, weekdays, hours, a minimum spend and a cap. The cashier sees which promotion applies.

  • For a product group, a category or single products
  • For one payment method, such as a bank card
  • Applied by itself or chosen by the cashier

Control and fraud watch

A supervisor PIN is needed for a big discount or a refund. A report per cashier shows discounts, refunds, cash differences and reprints, so you know where to look.

  • Supervisor PINs with a lock after wrong tries
  • Limits you set for discounts, refunds and cash differences
  • An event list to see what happened

Stock that follows every sale

Every sale takes stock out and every purchase brings it in. Units convert (a box of 12 pieces), and you can see what sells fast and what sits on the shelf.

  • Stock by warehouse and stock counts
  • Purchasing with unit conversions
  • Cost of goods sold and gross profit

Reports for the owner

Sales by day, week, month and year with growth against last month and last year, and an inventory report with cost and profit.

  • Sales dashboard and customer analysis
  • Inventory movement and cost of goods sold

Cost control

Food cost % for every dish against your target, alerts when ingredients get dearer, and records for waste, staff meals and stock count differences.

Guests order from the table

Every table has its own QR code. Guests scan it with their phone, see the menu with pictures, send their order, call the waiter or ask for the bill, with no app and no login. Staff accept the order and it goes straight to the kitchen or bar.

GoFood, GrabFood and ShopeeFood orders

The cashier types each order from the platform's tablet. The commission is worked out, what the platform owes you is booked as a receivable, and every payout is matched to the orders it paid. Orders are entered by hand, not imported automatically.

Control at the counter

A supervisor PIN is needed to void a dish the kitchen already has or to give a big discount. A report shows voids, discounts, reprints and cash differences per cashier, so you know where to look.

Reports for the owner

A restaurant dashboard with sales, average bill, sales by weekday and hour, top menu items and payment methods. It also shows your prime cost against a target, which dishes earn money (menu engineering), and a daily sales sheet split into food and beverage.

Staff roles and devices

Manager, cashier or waiter, kitchen and bar, and purchasing and stock roles, each with its own access. Every tablet or PC that logs in is registered as a device, and you can block one that is lost.

The back office is included

Ingredient purchasing with unit conversions (buy in kg, use in grams), suppliers, stock counts, prepared items such as sauces and doughs, accounting, bank reconciliation, budgets and tax, in the same system.

Loyalty members

Guests join as members at the cashier or by scanning a QR code, and earn points on every paid bill. Set your own tiers (for example Silver and Gold) with bigger rewards, let guests pay part of the bill with points, give a birthday dish, and set how long a membership lasts. Reports show your top members and points earned, used and expired.

Not included today: ordering from your own website or app, and automatic import of delivery-app orders (GoFood, GrabFood, ShopeeFood), which the cashier enters by hand. If you need them, tell us. Receipts, service charge and VAT should be checked with your tax advisor.
