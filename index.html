Questions we often hear

Do we need cloud hosting or a constant internet connection?

No. Innologic runs on a computer or small server in your office and staff use it through their browser over your network. Secure remote access can be arranged if you need to work from outside.

Can it handle several branches or companies?

Yes. Branches and departments can be tracked separately, and each company keeps its own books. Bills between your companies and group statements are supported.

Can we bring our existing products and customers?

Yes. We help you import products, customers, vendors and opening balances so you do not start from zero.

Is our data safe?

Users have roles and only see what they need, every change is recorded in an activity log, and two-factor login is available. We set up regular backups and recommend keeping copies on a second drive or cloud storage.

Is the tax output ready to file?

It records and exports the data you need for VAT, PPh and Coretax e-Faktur. Please always confirm rates and filing details with your tax advisor.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your business and how much setup and training you need. Send us a message and we will give you a clear quote.
